Where the house fits

The single-story layout and 2,110 sq ft support a premium over smaller three-bedroom homes. The original 2010 finishes and extensive carpet limit that premium. If the property becomes vacant in its current condition, our estimated re-leasing range is $2,700–$2,800 per month. A clean, show-ready home could open at $2,800. If the residents move

At a replacement rent of $2,800, each vacant day costs about $92 in uncollected rent. A 21-day gap leaves about $31,667 in first-year rent, roughly $2,533 less than a prompt $2,850 renewal, even before turnover expenses.

Owner decision

Authorize a 12-month renewal at $2,850 per month if a concession will help secure the residents. If they decline, inspect the carpet and finishes at turnover, get make-ready quotes, and revisit the two $2,800 competitors before listing. Reassess price and showing feedback during the first week.
